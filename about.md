		<p markdown="1">
			Cameron is an American painter born in Columbus, Ohio in 1995. Cameron graduated from Swarthmore College studying Computer Science and Mathematics before pursuing a formal training in academic drawing and painting, enrolling in the Florence Academy of Art in 2021. Upon his graduation in 2024, he was selected as a Graduate in Residence where he also studied etching under Daniel Graves. 
		</p>

		<p>
			The philosophy of Cameron's work is rooted in working from life, using intense observation in order to better connect with nature. Close investigation of a subject can reveal its essence, and its relationship to the human experience. Cameron hopes to further develop his own understanding of nature in a way that can others can connect to and see themselves in his work. 
		</p>
		
		<p>
			Cameron has exhibited his works in Europe and the US, and now lives and works in Florence as a Principal Instructor at the Florence Academy where he hopes to continue the realist tradition in painting and etching.
		</p>
